# 3D body assets — sources and licence

`ios/Blith/Resources/Body3D/body.bin` and `body3d.json` are generated by `build_body.py` from:

| Layer | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Skin (figure) | BodyParts3D 4.0, element FJ2810 "Skin" (FMA7163), IS-A tree OBJ, 99 % reduction — https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/ | CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan (notice in every OBJ file; the database site has listed CC BY 4.0 since 2025-02-27) |
| Bones (only used to derive regions and joint landmarks; not shipped) | BodyParts3D 4.0, "bone organ" elements | as above |
| Muscles, tendons, aponeuroses | Z-Anatomy `Resources/Models/FBX/MuscularSystem100.fbx`, itself a derivative of BodyParts3D — https://github.com/LluisV/Z-Anatomy | CC BY-SA 4.0 |

Required attribution (as specified by the sources):

> "BodyParts3D, (c) The Database Center for Life Science licensed under CC Attribution-Share Alike 2.1 Japan"
>
> "Z-Anatomy - The open source atlas of anatomy - CC-BY-SA 4.0"

The derived meshes (transformed into one frame, decimated, genital area replaced by a smooth neutral
surface, muscles snapped beneath the skin, an added underlayer surface) are distributed under
**CC BY-SA 4.0**. The app shows the credit under the body map (`BodyView`); keep it if the view is
restyled. The same strings are in `body3d.json` (`source`, `license`, `attribution`).

Raw source archives are cached outside the repository (`$BLITH_BODY_CACHE`, default
`~/.cache/blith-body`) and must not be committed.
