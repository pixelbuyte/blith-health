# Blith design language

## What the first version got wrong (from the screenshots)

| Screen | Problem | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Today | A bare step count in a white card; the insight read like a label; large empty areas; the sample banner dominated. | A cobalt **story card** leads with a sentence, today's steps and a same-time comparison curve. One **featured insight** has its evidence chart and a mascot. Then rhythm, milestones and context. The sample label is a small amber pill. |
| Walking | "0 steps today" dominated even with months of history below; the chart didn't say what it compared against. | Each range gets its **own headline and story**, a daily average, the comparison period and its dates. The chart shows avg and "before" lines, **measured zeros vs missing records**, and a tap-to-inspect **day panel** (steps vs usual weekday, workouts, body notes). Facts sit in **Measured**; interpretations in **What it means**. |
| Ask | A generic chat with plain cards. | Serif header with Bli listening or thinking; cards tinted by meaning; new day and body-note cards; **Why you're seeing this** on every answer. |
| Everywhere | Identical white rectangles; lifeless palette; stock SF Symbols. | Semantic palette, New York serif stories, SF Mono labels, tinted and hero cards, and an original symbol set. |

## Tokens (`ios/Blith/DesignSystem/Theme.swift`)

| Token | Meaning | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|---|
| cobalt | walking and movement, the brand | #2F5BFF | #5B82FF |
| cyan | "now", highlights, the assistant | #14AEE3 | #4FD6FF |
| teal (`sleep`) | sleep and recovery | #0E9E90 | #35D0C0 |
| coral (`note`) | the person's own body notes | #EE5A3A | #FF8466 |
| amber (`review`) | worth reviewing: sample or stale data | #C77E00 | #FFB23F |
| violet (`weight`) | weight | #6246F0 | #9A86FF |
| ink / paper | text / background | #0B1230 / #F2F5FC | #EEF2FF / #060A1A |

Type: New York serif (`Typo.display`, `Typo.story`) for sentences that interpret; SF Rounded
for numbers; SF Mono caps with tracking (`Eyebrow`) for labels. All are text-style based, so
Dynamic Type scales them. Deltas are neutral: arrows and numbers, never red/green verdicts.

## Custom assets

- **Symbols** (`Assets.xcassets/Symbols`, preview `design/symbols-preview.png`): 24 pt grid,
  1.8 stroke, round caps, template SVGs with filled tab variants: today, walk, body, ask,
  steptrail, sleep, bodynote, calendar, progress, sparkle, weight, streak, medal, heart, evidence.
- **Bli, the mascot** (`design/mascot/model-sheet.png`, `DesignSystem/Mascot.swift`): one egg
  silhouette with a cyan crown curl and one face. Poses (idle, waving, walking, thinking,
  listening, sleeping, noticing, celebrating, pointing) only move limbs, eyes and props. It is
  drawn and animated on device from the model-sheet geometry, pauses off screen, and respects
  Reduce Motion. It appears only on onboarding, import, empty states, the featured insight,
  the streak and Ask.
- **Body figure** (`Assets.xcassets/Body`, `design/body/`): generated by
  `design/body/generate_body.py`, a lean luminous-cobalt figure (front and back) with light
  definition and understated pelvis and glutes, plus `body-regions.json` anchors.
- **Walking signature**: generative art from the person's own hour-by-weekday steps.

## Interactions

- **Tap to inspect**: story card → Walk (day); insight → "Why" sheet or the exact records; bar →
  day panel; day panel note → Body; chat card → the same view, range and record.
- **Body**:
  - A short reveal, then barely perceptible breathing and a slow light sweep, paused off
    screen and under Reduce Motion.
  - Drag turns the figure between front and back; there are Front and Back shortcuts.
  - Pinch or the ± buttons zoom and centre on the selected region.
  - Tapping a region highlights it and opens a panel.
  - Selecting a note turns toward its region, zooms and shows its date.
  - The timeline slider shows notes that were unresolved on a chosen date. It never implies
    the body was reconstructed for that date.
  - A region list is the accessible alternative to tapping the figure.
- **Streak**: one check-in per day the app is opened. Missing a day ends the run but never
  touches the total, the best run or any milestone.

## Still interim

- **True 3D rotation**: this needs a licensed, rigged human mesh (USDZ or glTF, tasteful,
  matching the reference proportions) with a region map. Notes are anchored by region id, so
  they carry over unchanged.
- **The red muscle layer**: this should come from anatomically reviewed artwork that matches
  the blue figure's proportions and camera. It is not included yet on purpose.
